<section class="section">

    <p class="mb-2">
      If you would like to plan a trip to India, please reach out to Hannah Kim (<a href="mailto:hannah@changeforindia.net">hannah@changeforindia.net</a>) and she can add you or your group to the next trip planned. Please see below for trip details.
    </p>
    <p class="mb-2">
      Flight departs Friday evening with a stopover in Dubai/Seoul/Frankfurt/Singapore and land in Hyderabad Sunday early morning. From Hyderabad, we take a 3&frac12; hour car ride to our hotel in Miryalaguda. We take the rest of Sunday to get acclimated and prepped for our first meeting that evening.
    </p>
    <p class="mb-2">
      Medical, Dental, Vision clinics and Vacation Bible School runs during the week days, Monday-Friday. Evening evangelism meetings run from Sunday evening to Friday/Saturday evening. Saturday morning worship service is held at the school. All flights depart Saturday evening and arrive back in the states by Sunday afternoon.
    </p>

<!-- Preparation -->
<section class="section section-alt">
  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="mb-3">Preparation</h2>
    <div class="grid grid-2">
      <ul class="list-styled">
        <li><strong>Visa application</strong> &ndash; Need to receive a travel visa to India prior to trip. It takes approximately 3-4 weeks so sooner the better. Details on what to put on your form will be emailed out.</li>
        <li><strong>Immunizations</strong> &ndash; Need to locate a travel clinic to determine what immunizations are advised by the state department.</li>
        <li><strong>Medical supplies</strong> &ndash; We take with us one day's worth of medication and purchase the rest in India.</li>
        <li><strong>Dental supplies</strong> &ndash; We ask for donations from surrounding dentists &amp; friends. All dental supplies we take from the states.</li>
      </ul>
      <ul class="list-styled">
        <li><strong>Dental chair</strong> &ndash; Need to secure dental chairs depending on the number of dentists traveling.</li>
        <li><strong>Books, clothing, school supplies, sports equipment</strong> &ndash; Everyone takes 2 suitcases stocked with supplies and donated items. We try to limit personal items to take as much as we can to donate.</li>
        <li><strong>Sermons, Children's Story, Health Talk, VBS, Praise Music</strong> &ndash; All are advised to prepare presentations well in advance. We have PowerPoint sermons, health talks, and children's stories that can be distributed in advance. Our goal is to be ready before we get to India. Training sessions will be provided prior to the trip.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
