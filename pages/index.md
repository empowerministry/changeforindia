<section class="section">

    <h2 class="mb-2">About</h2>
    <p class="mb-2">
      <strong>Change for India</strong> invites anyone with a willing heart to join and give back to those less fortunate in India through a transformative 10-day short-term mission trip or an extended trip during the summer.
    </p>
    <p class="mb-2">
      Our mission is to bring hope and make a lasting difference in the lives of students and villagers living in rural farming villages by providing:
    </p>
    <ul class="list-styled mb-3">
      <li><strong>Christian Education &amp; Innovative Programming</strong></li>
      <li><strong>Health Clinics - Medical, Dental, &amp; Vision</strong></li>
      <li><strong>Evangelism - Children's Program, Prayer Walks, and Evangelistic Meetings</strong></li>
    </ul>
    <p class="mb-2">
      The trips focus on villages near Miryalaguda, Andhra Pradesh (about 3&frac12; hours east of Hyderabad), centered around the Bob Robinson Memorial SDA School&mdash;a K&ndash;10 Christian boarding school founded in 2007. For many villagers, this will be their very first encounter with foreign visitors and with Christian missionaries.
    </p>

    <blockquote class="quote">
      "A life not lived for others is not a life."
      <span class="quote-author">&mdash; Mother Teresa</span>
    </blockquote>

    <p class="mb-2">
      To get involved, visit <a href="/get-involved/"><strong>Trip Details</strong></a>&mdash;and join us for a life-changing adventure!
    </p>
