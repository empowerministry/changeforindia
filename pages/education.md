<!-- About the School -->
<section class="section">
  <div class="container">
    <h2 class="mb-2">About the School</h2>
    <p class="mb-2">
      The Bob Robinson Memorial SDA School (K&ndash;10 boarding school) was founded in 2007 to provide quality Christian education to children from impoverished rural villages. Students are carefully selected from the most economically and socially disadvantaged families in surrounding communities, and each one is fully sponsored by generous donors from around the world.
    </p>
    <p class="mb-2">
      At the height of enrollment (pre-pandemic), over 500 students called the school home. Currently, we have over 200 students and are adding 100 more students in the 2025-26 school year. The 13-acre campus includes a K&ndash;12 academic building, separate boys' and girls' dormitories, a cafeteria, faculty housing, and a church. Our vision is to grow enrollment to 1,500 students and expand to a college campus.
    </p>
    <p class="mb-2">
      A key focus of the school is its commitment to innovation and sustainability as we bring more innovative education programs and make plans to cultivate the land providing both food for the students and opportunities for a work-study program. In addition, we are increasing enrollment for local day students whose tuition will help offset operational costs.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>
