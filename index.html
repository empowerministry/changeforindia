About

Change for India invites anyone with a willing heart to join and give back to those less fortunate in India through a transformative 10-day short-term mission trip or an extended trip during the summer.

Our mission is to bring hope and make a lasting difference in the lives of students and villagers living in rural farming villages by providing:

  • Christian Education & Innovative Programming
  • Health Clinics - Medical, Dental, & Vision
  • Evangelism - Children's Program, Prayer Walks, and Evangelistic Meetings

The trips focus on villages near Miryalaguda, Andhra Pradesh (about 3½ hours east of Hyderabad), centered around the Bob Robinson Memorial SDA School—a K–10 Christian boarding school founded in 2007. For many villagers, this will be their very first encounter with foreign visitors and with Christian missionaries.

"A life not lived for others is not a life." — Mother Teresa
