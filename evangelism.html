Evangelism

Sharing hope and faith with rural communities

Evening Evangelistic Meetings

One of the most powerful highlights of the trip is the evangelistic meetings. Six months before our team arrives in India, local Bible workers are sent to the villages to "plant the seed." During this time, many are introduced to the gospel and receive Bible studies, preparing their hearts for the message of Christ.

When our team arrives, we lead a final evangelism series—summarizing what they've learned and inviting them to commit their lives to God. These meetings are held every evening, Monday through Saturday, from 7:00–10:00 p.m., and are structured into four sessions:

The Four Sessions

👶

Children's Program

Teaching songs and sharing Bible stories (often acted out) for children and families.

🩹

Health Education

A presentation on preventative health by a healthcare professional on basic hygiene and care.

📖

Message

A sermon covering the basics of Christianity and God's grace.

🙏

Prayer

Almost all who attend request personal prayer at the close of the evening.

The week concludes with a joyful baptism celebration for those who have decided to follow Christ. Seeing their earnest hearts and radiant joy is deeply humbling—reminding us that true happiness is found in God alone.
